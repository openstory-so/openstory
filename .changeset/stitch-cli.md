'@openstory/stitch': minor

Rename the package from @openstory/stitch-player. `npx @openstory/stitch` plays local stills and videos in the browser, and `stitch skill` installs the /stitch command. The preview frame is the canvas box of the clips, so a portrait cut fills the window as portrait. `--subs` takes one `.srt` or `.vtt` for the whole cut. The page can turn the music off, and it shows the same loading line and playback warnings as the theatre.
