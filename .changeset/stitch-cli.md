Rename the package from @openstory/stitch-player. `npx @openstory/stitch` plays local stills and videos in the browser, and `stitch skill` installs the /stitch command plus the stitch-player contract into `.agents/skills` and `.claude/skills`. The preview frame is the canvas box of the clips, so a portrait cut fills the window as portrait. `--subs` takes one `.srt` or `.vtt` for the whole cut. The page shows a loading line, warns about silent or missing media and mixed resolutions, and has icon buttons for music and Download. The package README is also in Chinese, French, and Spanish.
